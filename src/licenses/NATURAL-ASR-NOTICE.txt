Natural transcription uses Qwen3-ASR 1.7B (Apache-2.0), SpeechBrain VoxLingua107 ECAPA (Apache-2.0), and transcribe.cpp 0.4.0 (MIT).

Models run locally. Tested GGUF conversions by handy-computer:
